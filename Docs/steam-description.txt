[b]Start a new game with a random starting site, ideoligion and colonists at the press of one button.[/b]

You configure the game. Just Start deals you the hand.

One of the hardest things in RimWorld is actually getting started. I spend ages picking the perfect colonists and agonizing over which tile to settle, so long that I barely get around to playing. Maybe you do too. Just Start fixes that. Set up your game the way you like (scenario, storyteller, difficulty, world settings), then press one button. You get a random starting site, a random ideoligion and whoever the game gives you as colonists, and you're straight into the game.

[h2]Features[/h2]
Instead of Generate, click [b]Just Start[/b], which sits just to its left.
[list]
[*]Generates your world as usual.
[*]Picks a random starting site, weighted by biome like the "Select random site" button.
[*]Keeps the starting map chosen by scenarios such as The Gravship.
[*]Sets up your ideoligion, if you have Ideology.
[*]Starts the game with the scenario's colonists as they come, with the xenotype you chose if you have Biotech.
[/list]
You never see the site picker, the ideoligion screen or the colonist screen. Season and map size still come from the world settings page's Advanced settings.

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Right-click the Just Start button, or use the mod settings menu. Every filter is off by default, and [b]Reset to defaults[/b] turns them all off again.

[h3]Starting site[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Biomes[/b]: pick one or more biomes to start in, or leave them all unticked for any biome.
[*][b]Exclude extreme biomes[/b]: never start in a biome the game warns you about when settling, such as ice sheet, sea ice or extreme desert. The tooltip lists every biome this covers, including those from other mods.
[*][b]Allowed terrain[/b]: flat, small hills, large hills and mountainous.
[*][b]Required features[/b]: a river, a coast or a road.
[*][b]Growing period[/b]: the shortest outdoor growing period the site may have.
[*][b]Average temperature[/b]: the range the site's average yearly temperature must fall in.
[*][b]Hostile settlements[/b]: how far the site must be from any hostile faction's settlement.
[/list]

[h3]Ideoligion (Ideology only)[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Ideoligion mode[/b]: a classic ideoligion, a random fixed one, a random fluid one, or classic mode for every faction.
[*][b]Excluded memes[/b]: memes your random ideoligion never gets.
[/list]

[h3]Colonists[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Starting xenotype[/b] (Biotech only, Baseliner by default): every colonist gets the xenotype you pick, including your own custom xenotypes. Or choose [b]Mixed[/b] for a random xenotype for each colonist (optionally including custom xenotypes), or [b]Scenario's choice[/b] to keep the scenario's xenotypes, such as the sanguophage in The Sanguophage.
[*][b]Age[/b]: the biological age range of your colonists.
[*][b]Gender[/b]: any, balanced, all male or all female.
[*][b]Minimum passions[/b] and [b]Minimum major passions[/b] for each colonist.
[*][b]Excluded traits[/b]: traits your colonists never have.
[*][b]Capable of all work[/b], so nobody is incapable of anything, or just [b]Capable of violence[/b].
[*][b]No addictions[/b], [b]No pregnancies[/b] (Biotech only) and [b]No family or relationships[/b].
[/list]
Colonist filters apply to adult colonists. Children, babies and mutants a scenario asks for keep the scenario's settings. If a filter can't be met (say, a forced xenotype that can't do violence), the game gives up after a number of tries and you get the closest colonist it found.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Safe to add to an existing save. Just Start affects starting a new game.
